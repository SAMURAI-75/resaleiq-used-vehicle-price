The problem

Used two-wheeler prices in India are set by gut feel. Most research is on cars, and nobody has measured how electric scooters lose value against petrol ones.

What we built

Three price models — petrol two-wheelers, EVs and cars — behind one fast API, with a breakdown of what pushed the price up or down.

What's new

The first same-variant EV vs petrol depreciation study for Indian two-wheelers, plus a check on how state road tax carries into used prices.

How it works

From raw ads to a fair price.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Kaggle bike and car listings, 2026 BikeWale ads, and the official model lists of BikeWale and CarWale.

  2. 2

    Clean

    Drop duplicates, fix km and price outliers per model family and age.

  3. 3

    Engineer

    City → state, road-tax rate, model family, log of price as target.

  4. 4

    Train

    Linear Regression baseline, Random Forest and XGBoost on an 80/20 split.

  5. 5

    Explain

    Tree SHAP splits each prediction into the push from every feature.
